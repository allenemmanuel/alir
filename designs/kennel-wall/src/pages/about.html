We take in the animals nobody else will

ALIR is an independent, no-kill shelter that has run since 2009. We rescue injured, abandoned and abused cats and dogs, treat them, and care for them until they find a home. For some, that means for the rest of their lives.

Our name says who we are: ALIR stands for Animals Lovers Independent Rescuer.

How it started

Our founder and her partner grew up with dogs and cats all around them. In 2009 they found a puppy on a roadside in Kota Belud. They named her Chuggy, and the vet confirmed she had been born blind.

Caring for Chuggy changed their lives. They began taking in sick street dogs, old or ill pets whose owners wanted to abandon them or have them put down, disabled animals, and aggressive dogs with behaviour problems, which they trained with patience.

Since that first rescue, ALIR has rescued, treated and rehomed thousands of animals. Today we care for more than 300 cats and dogs at the shelter and in foster homes.

What we’re working towards

A world where every animal is treated with compassion and respect, cruelty is rare, and every creature gets a second chance.

  • Bring animal lovers together into a caring community.
  • Rescue unwanted, abandoned, sick, injured and lost animals.
  • Give them shelter and the best care we can, then find them safe, loving homes.
  • Promote animal welfare and responsible pet ownership through support, guidance and education.
