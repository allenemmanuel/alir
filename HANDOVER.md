# How to update the ALIR website

You do not need to write code or use any tools besides a web browser.

## Signing in

1. Go to **alirsabah.web.app/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and approve the request.
3. You will see the editor. The left side lists everything you can change.

## What you can change

| Section | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Contact, bank and PayPal details** | Phone numbers, email, bank account, PayPal link, and the urgent banner at the top of every page. |
| **Rescue stories** | The animal cards on the Rescues page. Add a card for each new rescue, or change its status (for example, from *In treatment* to *Recovered*). |
| **Shelter photos** | The photo gallery on the Shelter life page. |
| **In the news** | Newspaper articles. One article can be the featured story at the top. |
| **Press clippings** | Screenshots of news coverage, shown as a wall on the In the news page. |
| **Our team** | The people on the About page, with their photos and roles. |

## Making a change

1. Click the section you want (for example, **Rescue stories**).
2. To change an existing item, click it, edit the boxes, then click **Publish**.
3. To add a new item, click **New** (the button at the top of the list), fill in the boxes, then click **Publish**.
4. To remove an item, open it and choose **Delete entry**, then **Publish**.

Changes go live on the website about two minutes after you click **Publish**.

## Adding photos

Inside any box that asks for a photo, click **Choose an image**, then **Upload**. Pick a photo from your computer. Photos work best when they are landscape or square and under 2 MB.

## Good to know

- To write an article in the news section, you only fill in the headline, outlet, description, and link. You do not need to paste anything else.
- If something looks wrong on the live site after you publish, wait a few minutes and refresh the page. If it is still wrong, contact the website developer.
- The long text on the About, Donate and Shelter pages is not editable from this screen yet. Ask the developer to update it.
