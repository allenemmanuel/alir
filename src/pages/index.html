It started with a blind puppy on a roadside

“Saving one animal won’t change the world. But it changes the whole world for that one animal.”

In 2009 our founders found a puppy by the road in Kota Belud. They named her Chuggy, and the vet told them she had been born blind.

After Chuggy, they kept saying yes: to sick street dogs, to old pets whose owners wanted them put down, to disabled animals, and to dogs other people had given up on. That’s still how ALIR works today.
